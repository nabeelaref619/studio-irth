How many words fit in a one-minute video?

Estimate script duration, then calibrate the result to your actual voice.

Studio Irth · 12 October 2026

Estimate first, then record

There is no word count that guarantees a one-minute recording for every speaker and language. Pace, pauses, dialect, terminology and performance all change the result. Irth Tools estimates duration from your word count and selected speaking rate. It does not listen to audio or detect breathing, laughter or silent scenes.

Use a transparent calculation

Duration in seconds equals word count divided by words per minute, multiplied by 60. A 130-word script at 130 words per minute therefore takes 60 seconds before extra pauses. The default rate of 130 is an adjustable starting point, not a universal speaking-speed standard.

Calibrate with a short recording

Read a paragraph naturally and time the recording. If you speak 100 words in 50 seconds, your measured pace is approximately 120 words per minute. Enter that rate in the counter, then allow separate time for pauses and visual sequences. A conversational delivery may differ from narration even for the same speaker.

Understand the counting method

The counter uses your browser’s word segmentation when available. Punctuation alone is not counted as a word. Arabic affixes, numbers and abbreviations can be counted differently by different text editors. The character count uses visible grapheme clusters and includes spaces and punctuation, so it may differ from a platform’s own character limit.

Shorten the idea before speeding up

If a script runs long, remove repeated points and merge sentences that do the same work. Keep one main idea, one example and a clear closing line. Speaking faster to squeeze in more information can reduce clarity. Read the revised version aloud instead of relying only on the displayed estimate.

A practical short-video example

For a 60-second video, you might reserve time for a visual opening and a closing pause. If 50 seconds remain for speech and your measured pace is 120 words per minute, the calculation gives a target of about 100 words. This is a production estimate, not a formula for views. Record a take, check clarity and adjust the text.
