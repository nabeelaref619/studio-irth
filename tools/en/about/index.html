About Irth Tools

Practical creator tools from Studio Irth.

Make room for the work that matters

Irth Tools is a Studio Irth project led by Nabil Aref, bringing together simple utilities for preparing images and written content. We begin in Arabic and also provide English, because creative work often moves between both.

The project focuses on four jobs: image compression and resizing, image format conversion, word counts and speech estimates, and aspect ratio calculations. The tools work in your browser without an account.

Our approach to information

We explain the calculation and limitations of each tool, and distinguish illustrative examples from actual results. Content was prepared with AI assistance. The tools do not replace visual review or a timed recording. We welcome corrections.
