A few quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Open a tool and start. We do not ask for an email address to export results.

Where do my images and text go?

Our tool code processes files and text in your browser and does not send them to our server. Closing the page clears the working state. Hosting providers may process ordinary connection data as described in our privacy notice.

Will every image become smaller?

No. Some files are already optimised and may export larger. The tools show the actual output so you can choose what works.
