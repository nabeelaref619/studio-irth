Privacy notice

How Irth Tools handles files, text and usage data.

Last updated: 12 October 2026. Operator: Studio Irth / Nabil Aref. Use the contact page for enquiries.

Images and text

Our tool code processes your images and text in the browser and does not send their contents to our servers. We do not create user accounts or store input text and files in a database. Closing or reloading the page clears the working state. Downloaded files remain wherever you save them on your device.

Connection and hosting data

Loading the site sends ordinary connection data to the hosting provider. This may include IP address, browser type, requested path and operational or security logs. The primary site is hosted on GitHub Pages; a preview copy may use ChatGPT Sites. We do not specify the provider’s retention period here or claim full control over it.

Advertising and analytics

The primary site at studioirth.com uses Google AdSense to request advertising, subject to Google approving the site and suitable ads being available. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers and process information such as your IP address, browser, requested page and ad interactions to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised depending on your choices and applicable requirements. Our tool code does not send your input images or text to Google. We do not add Google Analytics. Preview copies on ChatGPT Sites do not load advertising code.

Advertising and privacy choices

For applicable visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. You can revisit your decision using the Privacy and cookie settings link that Google displays at the bottom of the page where applicable. Declining consent does not prevent you from using the tools. You may receive limited ads or no ads, depending on applicable requirements. Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google’s advertising technologies notice. You can also manage personalisation in My Ad Center.

US privacy choices

For visitors in supported US states, Google provides a Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link. Use it to opt out of sale or sharing for advertising. The tools remain available after opting out.

External links

Instagram and reference links take you to services with their own policies. We do not control information you share with them. Do not include sensitive information in feedback messages.

Privacy enquiries

To ask a question or report a concern, use the contact page. We cannot retrieve images or text that were never sent to our server.
