Privacy notice

How Irth Tools handles files, text and usage data.

Last updated: 12 October 2026. Operator: Studio Irth / Nabil Aref. Use the contact page for enquiries.

Images and text

Our tool code processes your images and text in the browser and does not send their contents to our servers. We do not create user accounts or store input text and files in a database. Closing or reloading the page clears the working state. Downloaded files remain wherever you save them on your device.

Connection and hosting data

Loading the site sends ordinary connection data to the hosting provider. This may include IP address, browser type, requested path and operational or security logs. The primary site is hosted on GitHub Pages; a preview copy may use ChatGPT Sites. We do not specify the provider’s retention period here or claim full control over it.

Advertising and analytics

The current version has no active Google AdSense code or third-party analytics tags. The tool code sets no advertising cookies and does not use local storage to retain your inputs. If advertising or analytics are added, we will update this notice and introduce the required choice and consent mechanisms before activation.

External links

Instagram and reference links take you to services with their own policies. We do not control information you share with them. Do not include sensitive information in feedback messages.

Privacy enquiries

To ask a question or report a concern, use the contact page. We cannot retrieve images or text that were never sent to our server.
