Count Arabic and English in one workspace

Paste a script or article to see words, characters and paragraphs. Blank lines separate paragraphs. Characters include spaces and punctuation; words use browser language segmentation, so results may differ slightly from Word or social platform limits.

How is speech duration calculated?

We divide the word count by your chosen speaking rate, then convert to minutes and seconds, rounding up. For example, 130 words at 130 words per minute take one minute before pauses. The default rate is a working assumption, not a measurement of your voice.

Turn an estimate into a filming plan

Record a short paragraph, measure your pace and update the setting. Allow time for pauses and visual scenes without speech. If the script is too long, remove repetition before increasing delivery speed. Text is not automatically saved; copy it to your own file before closing the page.
