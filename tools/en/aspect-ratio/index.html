Aspect ratio is not file size

A 16:9 ratio describes the relationship between width and height. Both 1920 × 1080 and 1280 × 720 have this ratio despite containing different pixel counts. This calculator returns dimensions only; it does not edit an image file or reduce its megabytes.

How height is calculated

Height equals your desired width multiplied by the height part of the ratio, divided by the width part. A width of 1080 with a 4:5 ratio produces a height of 1350. We round to the nearest whole pixel, so small differences can occur when the calculation does not divide evenly.

Choose a ratio for your composition

16:9 is landscape, 9:16 is portrait, and 1:1 is square. These are common geometric examples, not guarantees of any platform’s current requirements. Check your destination’s specifications. Do not stretch a picture to force another ratio; use cropping or borders when appropriate.
